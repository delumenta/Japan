AFTERNOON Higashiyama → Yasaka Pagoda

Afternoon · Higashiyama

~3:30–5:30 PM

⭐ PRIORITY
🏯

Yasaka Pagoda

The classic Higashiyama view. Spend some time around the streets surrounding Hōkan-ji and stay into golden hour if the light is good.

🌅 Best around sunset

AREA RECOMMENDATION
🏮

Ninenzaka

Traditional stone street with old wooden buildings, cafés, shops and classic Kyoto atmosphere.

🚶 Easy walk from Yasaka Pagoda

AREA RECOMMENDATION
🍵

Sannenzaka

Wander through the old streets, browse the small shops or stop for tea and snacks.

☕ Good for a short café stop

OPTIONAL Ishibe-koji · Quiet traditional lane near Gion

Evening · Gion

~5:30–7:30 PM

⭐ RECOMMENDED
⛩️

Yasaka Shrine

A great stop as the city changes from daylight into evening. The shrine remains atmospheric after dark.

🏮 Nice once the lanterns are lit

AREA RECOMMENDATION
🏘️

Hanamikoji Street

Walk through one of Gion's best-known traditional streets and surrounding side lanes.

🚶 Around 5–10 min from Yasaka Shrine

AREA RECOMMENDATION
🌙

Gion Shirakawa

Quieter canal-side streets, traditional buildings and one of the nicest evening walks in Gion.

📷 Great after sunset
